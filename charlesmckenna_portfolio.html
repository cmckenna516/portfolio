AI Toolbelt

The Tools I Actually Use

These aren't tools I've tried once. This is my working stack — the tools I reach for daily to build, automate, and operate. My honest take on each.

🧠
Claude
Primary AI

My daily driver for everything. I build Skills (automations), use Cowork mode for complex tasks, and have it connected to my calendar and email. Most of the builds in this portfolio started here.

💬
ChatGPT
Research & Ideation

Go-to for quick ideation, drafts, and broad research. Memory is genuinely useful. I bounce between ChatGPT and Claude depending on the task — they have different strengths. I also use it heavily for image generation: YouTube thumbnails, mocking up what a piece of furniture would look like in my actual living room, flyers for events, and other fun creative stuff. I've built custom GPTs here too — including Media Assistant, which takes a YouTube video description and generates optimized titles, descriptions, thumbnail concepts, and A/B test variants, building out the full asset suite around your main content.

⚡
Cursor
Code Editor

AI-native code editor. I'm not a classically trained software engineer but Cursor lets me build real things. Write a description of what you want, get working code. I also love that it shows you exactly which files are being touched — you can click into them and preview changes in real time. I run Claude Code inside Cursor too, which gives me an even tighter AI-assisted workflow when I need more control over the build.

🔁
n8n
Workflow Automation

Open-source automation that connects any system. I use it for multi-step workflows that run in the background — more powerful and flexible than anything else in its class.

🚀
Replit
Rapid Prototyping

Browser-based coding environment that removes every setup obstacle between you and a working app. I built a fully functioning project tracking tool for my team here — GANTT charts, tasks, subtasks, the works — without being asked. I saw a gap, filled it fast, and iterated on it with the team over time until it was exactly what they needed.

🌊
Parabola
Data Automation

No-code data pipeline tool with serious power under the hood. I built a flow that pulled from 30 different files across 2 separate data sources, combined everything into a single clean database, and then piped that database into Replit to power a visual dashboard built exactly around what the team needed to see.

🔗
Make.com
App Automation

Cross-app automation with a strong visual builder. More flexible than Zapier at the same price point. I use it to schedule automations and get complex, standardized processes to run reliably without anyone having to touch them — set it up once, let it run.

📓
NotebookLM
Research Synthesis

Ask a targeted question to one document or fifty, and get a straight, source-grounded answer. It's great for moments like "I know we have a policy on this but I can't remember the specific detail" — NotebookLM tells you the answer and shows you exactly where it came from. No hallucinating, no digging through files.

💎
Gemini
Multimodal AI

Google's AI with deep Workspace integration. The standout is that it's right where you're already working — no context switching, no copy-pasting into a separate chat. If your day lives in Drive, Docs, and Gmail, Gemini is just there. Pair it with Nano Banana for image work and it covers a lot of ground.

🍌
Nano Banana
Generative Assets

My go-to for image generation. When I need strong visual output fast — without a designer, without a brief, without waiting — Nano Banana delivers. Excellent image quality, quick iteration, and it handles creative prompts well.

What I'm Building Next

These are the ideas getting real time and real development effort. Each one is something I'd personally use — because that's the only products worth building.

Fit in a Pinch
Daily Workout Coach

A voice-first fitness app that has a short conversation with you each day to figure out the optimal workout, then logs it and tracks your progress over time.

03 · Productivity · Executive AI
Morning Briefing
AI Chief of Staff

An AI that wakes up before you do. Scans your calendar and email, preps your day, flags anything urgent or tied to a project you're watching — and removes friction before you even notice it.

  • Calendar-aware: meeting prep, attendee context, agendas
  • Email-aware: surfaces urgent items and watched-project updates
  • Proactive suggestions for date nights, workouts, errands
  • Connects to Gmail, Calendar, Slack, and fitness trackers
  • Always learning — gets more proactive the more you use it
04 · Career & Self-Discovery
TurasAI
AI Skill Discovery

An AI-guided skill discovery platform. It learns about you through conversation, generates hypotheses about where you may have real talent or potential, then assigns small experiments to test them — and scores your attempts with visible reasoning. The more you use it, the sharper it gets.

  • Conversational onboarding surfaces interests, energy patterns, and problem style
  • Generates skill hypotheses — not generic personality labels
  • Assigns small, low-cost experiments matched to your potential strengths
  • Scores attempts with a transparent rubric and plain-language feedback
  • Dashboard tracks signals over time and suggests what to try next
